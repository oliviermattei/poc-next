---
title: Why we deleted our queue
description: Three months into production, the queue cost more than it returned.
date: 2026-03-12
author: Olivier Mattei
tags: [engineering]
---

The queue had been added to absorb spikes. The spikes never came, and the queue produced everything a queue produces: duplicate messages, no ordering guarantee, and one more dashboard to watch.

Sending an email took 40 ms directly. Through the queue it took anywhere between 200 ms and eleven minutes.
