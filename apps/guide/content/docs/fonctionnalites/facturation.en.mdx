## In one sentence

The `billing` module lets your clients pay: monthly or yearly subscription, one-time purchase, trial period, billing by number of members, checkout without an account, and management of their payment method in the Stripe portal.

## What your clients see

| Screen | Address | What happens there |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | `/pricing` | compare plans, with or without an account, and subscribe |
| Settings › Billing | `/app/settings/billing` | see the subscription state, "Subscribe" to or "Buy" a plan, "Manage billing" (payment method, invoices and plan change, in the Stripe portal), see "Your purchases" |
| Reserved feature | `/app/premium` | the shipped example of a page reserved to paid plans: it invites those without access to subscribe |

When the `i18n` module is enabled, every address starts with the language (`/fr/…`, `/en/…`).

What your clients can go through:

- **Subscription states** shown on screen: "No subscription", "Trial period", "Active subscription", "Subscription cancelled" (access kept until the end of the paid period), "Payment failed", "Subscription expired".
- **Trial**: a plan can declare trial days (14 on the shipped plans). A trial is granted only once. Three days before it ends, the client receives the "Trial ending soon" notification, in the application and by email.
- **One-time purchase**: it does not expire and cannot be bought again ("Already purchased").
- **Who pays**: when an organization is chosen, the organization is subscribed, and only its owners and administrators manage billing. Without an organization, the account is.
- **Checkout without an account**: a visitor can subscribe from `/pricing` without being signed in. After paying, they receive an email: a link to choose their password if no account existed at that address, or a sign-in link if the account already existed.
- **Member cap**: a plan can limit the number of members of an organization; beyond it, an accepted invitation is refused, and nobody is removed.

### The shipped plans

Plans are declared in `config/billing.ts`. The shipped ones are **examples**:

| Identifier | Type | Displayed price | Trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pro-monthly` | monthly subscription | €29 per month | 14 days |
| `pro-yearly` | yearly subscription | €290 per year | 14 days |
| `lifetime` | one-time purchase | €490 | — |

The displayed price comes from `amount` and `currency`; **what is actually charged is the Stripe price referenced by `priceId`**. Replace the shipped `priceId` values (`price_pro_monthly`…) with those of your Stripe account: see [Plans and prices](/en/configurer/offres-et-prix).

## Enable or disable

```bash
pnpm ks toggle billing
```

- The module depends on no other module.
- **Disabled**: `/pricing` and Settings › Billing answer 404, no payment variable is required at startup, the "Your plan" step disappears from the welcome journey, the console revenue screen answers 404, and **every reserved feature is open to every account** (there is no plan left to reserve it). Tables already created are kept.

## Configure

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Plans, prices, trial, seats | `config/billing.ts` — [Plans and prices](/en/configurer/offres-et-prix) |
| Pages reserved to a plan | `config/gating.ts` — [Reserved features](/en/configurer/fonctionnalites-reservees) |
| The trial reminder text | `config/notifications.ts` — [Notifications](/en/configurer/notifications) |
| The payment provider | environment variables below — [Stripe](/en/services/stripe) |

You must **choose one of the two modes**, otherwise the application refuses to start and names the variables:

- **Local simulation**: `PAYMENTS_LOCAL_MODE=1` (the `.env.example` value). Payment is simulated by the application, nothing is sent to Stripe. It grants a subscription **without payment**: it is **refused in production**.
- **Stripe**: `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, and remove `PAYMENTS_LOCAL_MODE`. The webhook address to declare at Stripe is `<APP_URL>/api/modules/billing/webhook`.

A badly written plan in `config/billing.ts` stops the startup and names it.

To bring subscription state back in line with Stripe (for instance after a webhook outage), run:

```bash
pnpm billing:reconcile
```

It rewrites nothing when everything is already up to date, and never erases a subscription because Stripe is merely unavailable.

## Check that it works

In local simulation (`PAYMENTS_LOCAL_MODE=1`):

1. Create a new account and sign in (the demo accounts already have a subscription).
2. Open `http://localhost:3000/en/pricing`. You should see the three plans.
3. Click "Subscribe" on "Pro monthly". The browser goes through the simulated payment page, then comes back.
4. Open `/en/app/settings/billing`. The subscription should appear ("Trial period" or "Active subscription"), with the "Pro monthly" plan.
5. Open `/en/app/premium`: you should see the reserved feature, no longer the invitation to subscribe.

To check with real Stripe test keys, follow [Stripe](/en/services/stripe).

<Callout type="info" title="What is not included">
- **A single provider: Stripe.** Other payment providers are out of scope, as is **usage-based billing** (see [What the boilerplate does not do](/en/reference/ce-que-le-boilerplate-ne-fait-pas)).
- **Payment requires JavaScript** in the client’s browser; without it, the button stays disabled and a message says so.
- **No per-seat plan shipped** in `config/billing.ts` (all are `perSeat: false`), and no Stripe proration choice ([#102](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/102)).
- **Nothing checks** that `amount` and `currency` match the Stripe price of each `priceId` ([#103](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/103)).
- The `/pricing` page is not yet declared to search engines ([#104](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/104)).
- **The journey has not yet been run end to end with real Stripe keys** (one-time purchase, refunds, trial, seats, portal): [#106](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/106), [#107](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/107).
- When a client deletes their account, the seat count of their organizations is corrected at Stripe only at the next `pnpm billing:reconcile` ([#100](https://github.com/oliviermattei/poc-next/issues/100)).
</Callout>
