This Guide is for you, the **project owner**: you have the boilerplate in hand and you want to turn it into your SaaS. It explains what the boilerplate does, how to install it, configure it, enable or disable each feature, connect each service and take it to production.

In this Guide, a **client** is a person who uses your SaaS once it is online.

<Callout type="info" title="The Guide is not the product documentation">
The Guide is written for you. The [product documentation](/en/fonctionnalites/documentation-produit), served under `/docs` by your application, is the one **you** write for your clients. The two never mix: nothing in this Guide appears in your application.
</Callout>

## Where to begin

If the project is new to you, follow these four pages in order. Allow about half an hour.

1. [Install the project](/en/demarrer/installer): from nothing to an application running on your computer, without any provider key.
2. [Take a first tour](/en/demarrer/premier-tour): the demo accounts, the site, the application and the console.
3. [Understand the repository layout](/en/demarrer/structure): what you edit, and what you leave as it is.
4. [Work with an AI assistant](/en/demarrer/travailler-avec-une-ia): what your assistant must read before changing the code.

## Reading the Guide

- **Locally**: from the root of the repository, run `pnpm guide:dev`, then open `http://localhost:3200/en`. The `pnpm dev` command, which starts the application, also starts the Guide, at the same address.
- **In French**: every page exists in both languages, at the same addresses under `/fr`. The French version is the reference.
- **With an AI assistant**: the whole Guide fits in a single text file, `http://localhost:3200/en/llms-full.txt` (in French: `http://localhost:3200/llms-full.txt`). Give it to your assistant so it answers from the real documentation. The index of the pages is at `http://localhost:3200/llms.txt`.
